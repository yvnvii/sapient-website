Sketching Skulls is back, and spookier than ever! *Sapient* is hosting a Halloween sketching event on **October 27**! Stop by to look at, sketch, and paint REAL and FAKE primate bones and hominin casts. There will also be **FREE BRUNCH**—bagels, schmears, and fresh fruit—so bring your friends!

**What: Sketching skulls, eating candy, munchin' on brunch**

**When: Sunday, October 27, from 1-3 pm**

**Where: 865 Schermerhorn Ext (the Bone Zone)**

We hope to see you there!!
