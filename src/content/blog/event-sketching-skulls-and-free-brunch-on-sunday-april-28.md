Want to check out REAL human, primate, and in-between skulls? Fascinated with the macabre? WANT FREE BRUNCH?? Whether you have never picked up a pencil before in your life or are literally Pablo Picasso, come to Schermerhorn to see this not-to-be-missed live drawing event!!

![](/images/posts/event-sketching-skulls-and-free-brunch-on-sunday-april-28-1.png)

Sapient, Columbia’s undergraduate journal of biological anthropology, is hosting a sketching event in the bone lab (865 Schermerhorn Extension) on Sunday, April 28! Stop by from 1-3pm to sketch and paint primate bones and hominin casts. Free brunch—bagels, schmears, and fresh fruit—will be provided. Art materials will also be available, but attendees are more than welcome to bring their own supplies (and their friends)!

What: Sketching hominin skulls and eating brunch

When: Sunday, April 28, from 1-3 pm

Where: 865 Schermerhorn Extension

We hope to see you there!!
